Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well mixed.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the omelette: Pour the mixture into the pan. Tilt to spread evenly.
  4. Add fillings (optional): When edges are set, add your chosen fillings.
  5. Fold and serve: Fold the omelette carefully and cook 1 minute more.
  6. Enjoy! Serve hot with salt & pepper if needed.
