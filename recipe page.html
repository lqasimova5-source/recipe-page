Simple Omelette Recipe

A quick and delicious classic omelette made with simple ingredients. Perfect for breakfast, lunch, or a light dinner.

Prep Time 5 min
Cook Time 5 min
Total Time 10 min
Servings 1

Ingredients

Everything you need to prepare a simple and flavorful omelette.

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil
  • ¼ cup grated cheese (optional)
  • Chopped vegetables (optional)
  • Fresh herbs (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the eggs

    Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add salt and pepper, then whisk until the mixture is smooth and well combined.

  2. Heat the pan

    Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Add the butter or olive oil and allow it to heat gently.

  3. Cook the omelette

    Pour the egg mixture into the pan and gently tilt the pan so the eggs spread evenly across the surface.

  4. Add the fillings

    When the eggs begin to set, add cheese, vegetables, herbs, or your preferred fillings to one side.

  5. Fold and finish

    Carefully fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute until the inside is fully set.

  6. Serve

    Transfer the omelette to a plate and serve immediately. Add fresh herbs or additional seasoning if desired.

Chef's Tip

Cook the omelette over medium heat rather than high heat. This helps keep the eggs soft and prevents the outside from browning too quickly.
